# OnlineResturantPlatformAnalysis
This project provides a comprehensive data analysis of a restaurant platform's operational efficiency.
<br>
<br>

This project provides a comprehensive data analysis of a restaurant platform's operational efficiency. Using Tableau, I processed raw transactional data to identify key performance drivers, customer churn patterns, and retention strategies to provide actionable insights for stakeholders.

<br>
<br>
🎯 # Project Aim
<br>
The primary goal of this analysis was to evaluate the platform's health by:

Measuring Efficiency: Identifying top-performing restaurant partners and high-demand items.

Analyzing Retention: Understanding customer loyalty and repeat order behavior.

Reducing Churn: Pinpointing where customers drop off and suggesting improvements to enhance the lifetime value (LTV).

<br>
<br>
📊 # Key Insights from Dashboards
<br>
Based on the visualized data, the following areas were analyzed:

Top Sellers & Revenue: Identification of "Star" items and high-performing franchises (e.g., McDonald's, KFC) to optimize supply chains.

Geospatial Analysis: A "Customer Per City" map visualization to identify high-density delivery zones and underserved areas.

Order Status Trends: Tracking "Delivered vs. Cancelled" orders over time to measure service reliability.

Revenue Growth: Analyzing month-over-month revenue trends to detect seasonal fluctuations.


<br>
<br>
🛠️ # Data Pipeline:
<br>
Data Cleaning: Handled missing values and standardized restaurant names/categories.

Calculated Fields: Created custom metrics for Churn Rate and Retention Rate %.

Dashboarding: Built interactive Tableau visualizations including dual-axis charts, heat maps, and trend lines.

Efficiency Audit: Evaluated delivery times and order fulfillment rates.

Remaining Steps:
Cohort Analysis: Segmenting customers by their "Join Date" to see how retention evolves over 6–12 months.

Predictive Modeling: Integrating a Python/R script to predict which customers are "At Risk" of churning based on their recent activity.

A/B Testing Simulation: Evaluating the potential impact of loyalty discounts on the current churn rate.

💡 # Improvements & Suggestions
<br>
To improve platform efficiency and retention, the following recommendations were presented to the client:
Improve delivery time by finding the bottleneck

Loyalty Programs: Introduce tiered rewards for the "Top Stories" (frequent users) identified in the bar charts.

Operational Optimization: Focus on the "Delivery Status Over Time" metrics to reduce delays in specific low-performing zones.

Menu Engineering: Promote high-margin items that currently have lower visibility but high retention potential.


🏁 Conclusion
The analysis reveals a strong correlation between delivery speed and customer retention. While the platform shows steady growth in specific regions, the Churn Rate spikes in areas with inconsistent delivery statuses. By implementing the suggested loyalty interventions and focusing on high-performing city clusters, the platform can expect a significant increase in customer lifetime value.
